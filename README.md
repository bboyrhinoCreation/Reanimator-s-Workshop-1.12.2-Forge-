# 🚀 Reanimator's Workshop

A custom-designed modification for Minecraft Java Edition (1.12.2) [Forge].

This project features unique gameplay progression, custom mechanics, and entirely tailored environments, fully conceptualized and designed from the ground up. Harvest body parts from the dead, stitch them into undead servants on a galvanic slab, throw the lever, and build a gothic laboratory empire worked by your own cadavers.

---

## ✨ Features
*   **Stitch Your Own Undead:** Every mob can drop body parts with random stats and rarity. Examine them, lay them out on the Reanimation Slab, and throw the Galvanic Lever. The brain decides what rises: Thralls, Bone Servants, Crawlers, Stalkers, Hounds, Steeds, and the many-mobbed Amalgam.
*   **A Cadaver Workforce:** Put your creations to work as Miners, Quarrymen, Farmers, Lumberjacks, Foresters, Herders, Haulers, Crafters, Foundrymen and Builders. Each calling has its own skill tree, and Stalker Haulers can reach across dimensions.
*   **Combat Classes & Skill Trees:** Every creature type has its own fighting classes with signature abilities, augments and traits. Your own Necromancer tree in the Reanimator's Journal raises how many cadavers you command.
*   **Great Machines of the Laboratory:** Gothic multiblocks shown layer by layer in the Architect's Folio: the Reanimation Altar, Dissection Theatre, Galvanic Infuser, Rendering Vat, Grafting Crucible, Mnemonic Engine and the Ringmaster's Arena.
*   **The Foundry & Logistics:** An Ore Furnace and Alloy Vat that cast molten metal through taps, channels and moulds. A Stamp Mill, a networked Storage Terminal, tanks, pipes, the Great Cistern, and an Infernal Pump that draws lava from the Nether forever.
*   **Grafted Gear:** Tools, weapons, armour, bows and the Tesla Gun, built from frames, bindings and grips cast in over thirty metals. Each piece levels up and grows its own skill tree, and it never breaks.
*   **Graveyards, the Gloaming & Grave Sovereigns:** Graveyard biomes haunted by ghosts and banshees, the ever-dark Gloaming and the Ossuarium dimensions, the Horde at nightfall, and named boss Sovereigns summoned at a Profane Reliquary.
*   **Gothic Building:** Gothic and mausoleum stone, marble masonry with self-shaping pillars, connected-texture panelling, laboratory floors and glass in sixteen colours, tube lights, neon, braziers, tesla coils and more.
*   **A Full In-Game Guide:** The Necro-Galvanic Compendium explains every block, machine and number, with JEI support throughout.

## 🛠️ Installation & Requirements
*   **Minecraft Version:** 1.12.2
*   **Mod Loader:** Forge (14.23.5.2860 or newer recommended)
*   **Dependencies:** None required. Optional support for JEI, Just Enough Resources, Tinkers' Construct, Construct's Armory and Pam's HarvestCraft. Each switches on only when that mod is installed.

Simply download the compiled `.jar` file from our official releases pages (https://bboyrhinocreation.itch.io/reanimators-workshop))  or in this repository under releases and drop it into your instance's `mods` folder.
